# Start here

## What OCF is

Open Consciousness Functions (OCF) is an open-source guided audio project.
Its current functions help you explore a focused practice through a guided
session, then use the learned cue and its instructions in everyday life.
Standalone guided meditations and connected series are planned too.

[How OCF works](../how-it-works/) explains the guidance, cues and stereo sound.
[See the two practice paths below](#choose-your-path), or
[check your left and right audio](../listening/#stereo-test) before listening.

## Preparation Learning

Complete [Preparation Learning](../preparation-learning/) before using any OCF
function. Only people who have already learned the official Human Plus
preparation track may skip it. Preparation Learning is a separate introductory
session, not one of the {{function_count}} function records. Its own published edition and
matching transcript appear on its page when present in the verified release
catalog.

## Choose one practice

Choose a function that fits what you want to explore. Read its guide, then listen somewhere you can give the session your attention. Do not listen while driving, operating machinery, or doing anything else that requires your attention.

## A suggested way to explore

After Preparation Learning, these five functions can be approachable starting
points:

1. [Relax — OCF-FND-004](../functions/OCF-FND-004/) — a gentle introduction to
   guided relaxation and the structure of OCF listening.
2. [Focus — OCF-FND-002](../functions/OCF-FND-002/) — an attention-oriented
   guided practice and a way to become familiar with its documented cues.
3. [Imprint — OCF-COG-004](../functions/OCF-COG-004/) — a practice for capturing
   selected information or experiences you want to retain.
4. [Recall — OCF-COG-006](../functions/OCF-COG-006/) — a practice for bringing
   learned material or memories to mind.
5. [Think Fast — OCF-COG-007](../functions/OCF-COG-007/) — a thinking-oriented
   practice to explore through its own documented instructions.

You can explore these functions in any order; the list is an optional browsing
suggestion, not a required sequence among them. Reading a guide does not mean an
audio recording is available.

For optional sequences organized around learning, daily life, rest, and
reflection, explore [Function Chains](../function-chains/).

## Learn the cues, then choose

The [quick reference](../quick-reference/) lists current cues. A cue
identifies a command; it does not replace the documented steps. Browse
[all functions](../functions/) by canonical category, or review
the [listening guide](../listening/) and your [device's audio settings](../listening/).

## After listening

There is no required report or universal schedule. Use only the function's
documented instructions, and stop if uncomfortable. OCF is not a substitute for
healthcare, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency help.
