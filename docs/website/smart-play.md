## Smart Play

Smart Play lets you change **parts** of a session without changing the original
recording. It is optional. The player starts with **Original recording** unless
you save a different choice.

1. Open an eligible edition 0.3.0 recording from [Browse & Listen](../functions/)
   or from a function guide's **Listen and download** section.
2. Open **Smart Play settings**, the gear next to the seek bar. Choose **Full
   guidance**, **Shorter guidance**, or **Custom** to select guidance section by
   section. Only options available for that recording are offered. Unchanged
   sections use the original recording.
3. Choose **This play only**, **Remember for this session**, or **Set as default
   for all sessions**. Select **Apply**. If you change the audio, it restarts
   paused; select **Play** when ready.

When your choices change the audio, **Smart Play bookmarks** appear under the
player. Open them to jump to just before a changed section. Choose **Original
recording** in the gear menu to switch back. You can review, export, or reset
your default under **Preferences → Smart Play settings**. A saved session
choice takes precedence until you select **Use site default for this session**
in that player's settings and apply it. Saved choices stay in this browser.

## Common questions

### Why don't I see Smart Play settings or all the choices?

The gear is available for supported edition 0.3.0 function recordings.
Preparation Learning and older editions use Original recording only. If you
don't see the gear, check the **Audio edition** in Preferences and open the
player for the session you want. Not every session offers the same changes.
If your browser can’t load Smart Play, you can still play the Original recording.

### Does Smart Play change the files I download?

No. Downloads still provide the original MP3 or FLAC file. A Smart Play edit is
played in the site player as a single FLAC stream; edited selections aren't
separate downloads. Published transcripts and timed captions are for the
original recording, so timed captions aren't shown while an edit is active.

### What if a Smart Play selection doesn't play?

Open **Smart Play settings**, choose **Original recording**, and apply it to
listen without the edits. You can also download the original recording from the
session's **Listen and download** section.
