## Getting ready

### Preparation Learning

The starter recording that teaches the steps and cues used across OCF function sessions. Learn it before starting a function, unless you’ve already learned the official Human Plus preparation. [Preparation Learning](../preparation-learning/) · [Start Here](../start/)

### Transmutation Box

Imagine a box, or simply think of a place to set distractions aside for this session. You can come back to them later. Monroe’s related exercise is called the Energy Conversion Box. [OCF Preparation Learning](../preparation-learning/) · [Monroe Institute: Energy Conversion Box](https://support.monroeinstitute.org/support/solutions/articles/70000654367-emotional-responses-well-being-during-program-participation)

Picture setting your keys, wallet, phone or work phone in the box. You can also put in what’s on your mind: work drama, a limiting thought, or even the boogeyman. You don’t need to see a picture. A word, a symbol, or simply the idea of putting it aside works for the exercise.

During the box step, even in a short reminder, you can use a cue you already know as taught: [Plus Let Go](../functions/OCF-EMO-001/) for an unwanted emotion, or [Plus Offload](../functions/OCF-EMO-002/) for a distracting thought or recurring pattern. The box is still the idea of setting it aside for now.

### Relaxation

A guided step for settling your body and attention. The narrator may guide you through relaxing your body or offer a shorter reminder of a cue you’ve already learned. [OCF Preparation Learning](../preparation-learning/) · [Smart Play choices](../listening/#smart-play)

### Resonant Tuning

An optional breathing and sound exercise. In a guided version, follow the narrator’s timing and gently hum or tone as you breathe out. Some recordings skip it. [Try it in the OCF Energy Ball session](../functions/OCF-EXP-007/#audio-editions) · [Monroe Institute: free Resonant Tuning article](https://www.monroeinstitute.org/blogs/blog/how-resonant-tuning-helps-you-connect-more-easily-with-higher-states)

### Energy Ball

Imagine or sense a ball around yourself or a chosen space. You can rehearse expanding it, giving it qualities you choose, and drawing it back within. The dedicated Energy Ball session teaches the exercise and its Plus Inflate and Plus Deflate cues. Monroe’s related practice is called REBAL, short for Resonant Energy Balloon. [OCF Energy Ball guide](../functions/OCF-EXP-007/) · [Monroe Institute: REBAL practice](https://www.monroeinstitute.org/blogs/blog/tips-for-strengthening-expanding-and-using-your-biofield-with-the-resonant-energy-balloon-rebal)

**Aphantasia-friendly tip:** Think of the Energy Ball as spinning so fast it’s invisible. No picture needed. Anchor the idea to the feeling of being energized or powered up, if that helps.

If you like to visualize, give the ball vivid colors, light, or trails that show how it moves. You can use images, feelings, or both.

### Affirmation

A statement you repeat silently to set your intention for the practice. A recording may speak it in full, remind you to say it yourself, or skip it. [Smart Play guidance choices](../listening/#smart-play) · [OCF Affirmations](../affirmations/)

## Focus levels and session terms

### Focus levels

Labels the narrator uses to tell you what to do at different points in a session. A number identifies a stage in the guidance. It isn’t a frequency in hertz or a reading from an EEG. [OCF sound design and Focus stages](https://github.com/ocfproject-org/open-consciousness-functions/blob/3b9eabba815dfbde685a0bd01ea8560d9cbbb092/docs/SEMANTIC_DSP.md)

### Focus 10

The point where you let your body relax while staying aware enough to follow the guidance. You may hear this described as “mind awake, body asleep.” You aren’t asked to fall asleep here. [Monroe Institute: Mind Awake, Body Asleep](https://www.monroeinstitute.org/blogs/blog/back-to-the-basics-mind-awake-body-asleep) · [OCF Preparation Learning](../preparation-learning/)

### Focus 11

In OCF, the part of a function session where you learn and rehearse the function. The **Access** instructions guide you into and out of that part of the session. [OCF Energy Ball guide](../functions/OCF-EXP-007/) · [OCF session design](https://github.com/ocfproject-org/open-consciousness-functions/blob/3b9eabba815dfbde685a0bd01ea8560d9cbbb092/docs/SEMANTIC_DSP.md)

### Focus 20

A sleep-oriented stage used in some OCF recordings. The voice may get quieter and repeat instruction you’ve already heard. A recording can include Focus 20 and still guide you back to wakefulness at the end. [OCF audio pipeline: endings](https://github.com/ocfproject-org/open-consciousness-functions/blob/3b9eabba815dfbde685a0bd01ea8560d9cbbb092/docs/AUDIO_PIPELINE.md)

### Cue

A short phrase you link to a practice while learning it, like a human hotkey. Repeat the guided session if the cue doesn’t feel anchored yet. Once it works for you, think or say it silently as taught and give the familiar state time to return. You no longer need the training recording to use it. [How cues work](../how-it-works/#cues-are-like-human-hotkeys) · [Quick Reference](../quick-reference/)

### Waking return and sleep ending

A waking return guides you back to ordinary alertness, sometimes called **C1**, with a count or a shorter prompt. A sleep ending leaves space to drift off instead of including a wake-up sequence. These describe how a recording ends, not whether it includes a quiet section earlier. [OCF audio pipeline: endings](https://github.com/ocfproject-org/open-consciousness-functions/blob/3b9eabba815dfbde685a0bd01ea8560d9cbbb092/docs/AUDIO_PIPELINE.md)

## Common questions

### Where should I start?

Check the left and right channels, then follow [Start Here](../start/). After you’ve learned the preparation, pick one function and follow its guided track rather than trying to learn many cues at once. [Stereo check](../listening/#stereo-test)

### Do I need to see a mental picture?

No. OCF’s guidance is intentionally worded with aphantasia in mind, so you can follow it without seeing a mental picture. Use vivid images if you like them, or work with the idea, a feeling, or a mix of both. See the [Energy Ball example](#energy-ball) for a few ways to try it. Follow the words in the track in whatever way makes sense to you. [OCF Energy Ball guide](../functions/OCF-EXP-007/)

### Do I have to say the cue out loud?

No. Think it or say it silently in your mind when the function’s instructions call for it. Hearing the narrator say it isn’t the same as choosing to use it. [How OCF works](../how-it-works/) · [Example: Energy Ball cues](../functions/OCF-EXP-007/)

### Why did the voice get quiet?

Some recordings lower the narration when they repeat the earlier teaching in the rest section. You don’t need to turn up the volume just to follow that repetition. See [Typical function session](../how-it-works/#what-a-typical-function-session-looks-like) and the [Listening Guide](../listening/).

### Why should I use stereo headphones or earbuds?

They keep the left and right tones separate. That separation is how the binaural beat is presented. Use [Test Audio](../listening/#stereo-test) to hear each side on its own, or read [What the stereo audio does](../how-it-works/#what-the-stereo-audio-does) for the explanation.

### Can I shorten or skip a preparation step?

On eligible recordings, use the gear beside the player to see which sections you can change. Choose **Original recording** to hear the unchanged release. [Smart Play instructions and FAQ](../listening/#smart-play)
