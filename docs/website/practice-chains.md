# Function Chains

Explore seventeen optional sequences for learning, daily life, rest, and reflection.
Each step links to its function's guide and shows the current spoken cue.
Use a whole chain or choose individual practices. Mental pictures and particular
sensations are not required for these examples.

## Preparation first

Complete [Preparation Learning](../preparation-learning/) before using any OCF
function. Only people who have already learned the official Human Plus
preparation track may skip it.

## Learning

Relax → Focus → Think Fast → Imprint → Recall

1. [Relax](../functions/OCF-FND-004/) — Begin with physical and mental relaxation.
2. [Focus](../functions/OCF-FND-002/) — Direct attention toward the material you want to learn.
3. [Think Fast](../functions/OCF-COG-007/) — Explore thinking speed while working with that material.
4. [Imprint](../functions/OCF-COG-004/) — Choose the information or experience you want to retain.
5. [Recall](../functions/OCF-COG-006/) — Return to the material you want to bring to mind.

## Performance

Relax → Think Fast → Coordination → Focus

1. [Relax](../functions/OCF-FND-004/) — Begin with relaxation before approaching the task.
2. [Think Fast](../functions/OCF-COG-007/) — Explore thinking speed and mental processing for the task.
3. [Coordination](../functions/OCF-PRF-003/) — Turn to the practice concerned with steadiness and internal rhythm.
4. [Focus](../functions/OCF-FND-002/) — Direct attention toward the task you have chosen.

## Communication

Relax → Focus → Speak Up → Inner Power → Think Fast

1. [Relax](../functions/OCF-FND-004/) — Begin with physical and mental relaxation.
2. [Focus](../functions/OCF-FND-002/) — Bring attention to the conversation or message.
3. [Speak Up](../functions/OCF-COM-001/) — Explore clear, confident verbal expression.
4. [Inner Power](../functions/OCF-PRF-002/) — Reflect on the personal strength and resources you bring to the exchange.
5. [Think Fast](../functions/OCF-COG-007/) — Explore thinking speed as you consider what to say next.

## Emotional reset

Relax → Let Go → Reset

1. [Relax](../functions/OCF-FND-004/) — Begin with physical and mental relaxation.
2. [Let Go](../functions/OCF-EMO-001/) — Explore releasing clinging, tension, or an unwanted mental focus.
3. [Reset](../functions/OCF-FND-005/) — Turn toward renewed wakefulness, clarity, energy, and confidence.

## Decision

Relax → Let Go → Options → Manifest

1. [Relax](../functions/OCF-FND-004/) — Begin with physical and mental relaxation.
2. [Let Go](../functions/OCF-EMO-001/) — Explore releasing clinging, tension, or an unwanted mental focus.
3. [Options](../functions/OCF-COG-005/) — Consider the choices and alternatives available to you.
4. [Manifest](../functions/OCF-EXP-001/) — Clarify a future condition you want to work toward and its intended timing.

Optional variation: add [Contemplation](../functions/OCF-COG-003/) before Manifest for quiet reflection on the choices.

## Recovery

Relax → Body Tune-Up → Restorative Sleep

1. [Relax](../functions/OCF-FND-004/) — Begin with physical and mental relaxation.
2. [Body Tune-Up](../functions/OCF-BOD-013/) — Include a whole-body balance-oriented practice rather than one focused on a particular body area.
3. [Restorative Sleep](../functions/OCF-SLP-001/) — Make rest and recovery the purpose of the closing practice.

## Habits

Relax → Habit Freedom → Let Go → Offload

1. [Relax](../functions/OCF-FND-004/) — Begin with physical and mental relaxation.
2. [Habit Freedom](../functions/OCF-LIF-001/) — Identify the repeated behavior pattern you want to step away from.
3. [Let Go](../functions/OCF-EMO-001/) — Explore releasing clinging, tension, or an unwanted mental focus.
4. [Offload](../functions/OCF-EMO-002/) — Explore reducing interference from a recurring thought or emotional pattern while retaining useful memories.
